Working together

From discovery
to handover.

Start with a bounded change. Use the pilot to learn where agents help and where your team still needs to make the call. In two small pilots on Hivemill’s own backlog, a merged PR cost $1.33 and $4.64 in estimated API spend, against $3.56–14 for the old chain of agents.

  1. 01

    Find the right change.

    Choose one noncritical change in your repository, with test data, acceptance criteria and an engineer who owns the outcome.

  2. 02

    Build the pilot.

    Install the runner on your machine, write specs whose criteria name their proofs, and set a budget per task and Claude usage caps. Track estimated cost per merged PR during the run.

  3. 03

    Review the run.

    Inspect each pull request, the re-run proofs, the judge’s verdicts, cost and the time your engineers spent answering questions.

  4. 04

    Hand it over.

    Your team takes over the configured workflow and decides what to expand or refine.

At handover

What your
team keeps.

A running installation
The MIT-licensed runner on your laptop, homelab or servers. Claude Code continues to use its model provider.
Specs and proofs
Task specs for your repository, with a test or command proof for each criterion.
Independent checks
A gate that re-runs the proofs and a fresh judge that maps every change to your criteria before the merge.
Operating limits
Budgets, Claude usage caps, a morning digest and one queue for questions that need your team.
