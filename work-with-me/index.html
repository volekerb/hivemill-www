Working together

From discovery
to handover.

Start with a bounded change. Use the pilot to learn where agents help and where your team still needs to make the call.

  1. 01

    Find the right change.

    On a call, we look at your repository, machines and backlog. We choose a noncritical workflow with test data, such as validating an internal report, maintaining a client integration or improving an export.

  2. 02

    Build the pilot.

    We install Hivemill on your host. Together we define acceptance criteria, identify test commands, adapt the roles and skills, and set resource and spend limits for the run.

  3. 03

    Review the run.

    Trace the pull request through the reviewer, QA and judge. Inspect the findings, usage and time your engineers spent helping the work along.

  4. 04

    Hand it over.

    Your team takes over the configured workflow and decides whether to expand it, refine the roles or keep the pilot at its current scope.

At handover

What your
team keeps.

A running installation
An MIT-licensed factory on your Debian or Ubuntu infrastructure. Agent runtimes continue to use their model providers.
Roles and skills
A workflow assembled from the catalog and tailored to your repository.
Independent checks
A reviewer, QA and a judge-controlled merge gate, with the pilot’s acceptance criteria and test commands.
Operating limits
The resource and usage settings you selected, and a way to inspect costs, capacity and requests for attention.
